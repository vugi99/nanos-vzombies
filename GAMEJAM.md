# 🧟 vzombies

> **The ultimate round-based cooperative zombie survival experience for nanos world.**

---

## 📖 Short Description

**vzombies** is a fast-paced, action-packed round-based co-op zombie survival mode built from the ground up. Heavily inspired by iconic classic wave-defense shooters, it drops you (and up to your squad of survivors) into treacherous quarantine zones crawling with relentless undead. 

Scavenge points with every shot, unlock barricaded doorways, purchase weapons off the walls, gamble at the Mystery Box, and drink perk concoctions to power up. Whether you are holding out with friends or teaming up with intelligent AI bots, **vzombies** delivers adrenaline-fueled arcade action where teamwork, reflexes, and strategy are the only things keeping you alive.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### 1. The Core Objective
Survive as many escalating rounds of the undead horde as you can. Every round defeated brings faster, tougher, and more numerous enemies. If all players are downed, the game ends.

### 2. Basic Controls & Getting Started
* **Movement:** `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` to move, `Shift` to sprint, `Space` to jump, `C` / `Ctrl` to crouch or prone.
* **Combat:** `Left Click` to fire, `Right Click` to aim down sights (ADS), `R` to reload.
* **Interaction:** Press `E` (or your contextual interact key) to:
  * Rebuild broken window barricades.
  * Purchase wall weapons and ammo refills.
  * Roll the Mystery Box.
  * Buy and drink Perk Colas.
  * Turn on map Power and trigger Pack-a-Punch.
  * Revive fallen teammates before their bleedout timer expires.
* **Ping System:** Use the 3D ping key to highlight weapons, objectives, or zombie threats to your teammates.

### 3. Economy & Survival Strategy
* **Earn Cash:** Earn points for every body shot, lethal headshot, and knife kill.
* **Open the Map doors:** Spend points to open debris gates and locked doors to access power switches, perks, and better defenses.
* **Perks & Upgrades:** Prioritize defensive perks like **Juggernog** and **Quick Revive**, then upgrade your favorite weapons through the **Pack-a-Punch** machine for massive damage and elemental effects.

## 👁️ What You Will See in the Gamemode

* **Atmospheric Arenas:** Iconic survival maps (*Kino der Toten*, *Town*, *Supermarket*), each environment is filled with strategic choke points, secret rooms, and dynamic lighting.
* **Terrifying & Varied Threats:**
  * **Standard Walkers & Sprinters:** Fast-closing undead that dynamically vault fences, mantle obstacles, and claw through window frames.
  * **Crawlers & Dismemberment:** Precise shots blast off zombie arms, heads, and legs with dynamic ragdolls and crawling behaviors.
  * **Hellhounds:** High-speed, explosive canine rounds accompanied by ominous fog and lightning strikes.
  * **Brutus & Heavy Bosses:** Armored behemoths designed to shatter defensive lines.
  * **Napalm Zombies:** Blazing monstrosities that ignite surroundings and detonate upon defeat.
* **Visual & Audio Polish:** Glowing Mystery Box light beams piercing the sky, retro jingles echoing from perk machines, dynamic bullet impacts, floating point indicators, and responsive custom HUD overlays.
* **Wild Alternate Sub-Modes:**
  * 🎯 **Sharpshooter:** Random weapon cycling every 60 seconds with bottomless reserves.
  * 🔫 **Gun Game:** Level up your arsenal tier with every wave of kills.
  * 👻 **Invisible Zombies:** Rely entirely on sound direction, muzzle flashes, and radar cues to fight an invisible menace.
  * 🕹️ **Top-Down:** Transforms the game into an arcade twin-stick shooter with real-time camera zoom.
  * ⚔️ **Grief:** Competitive last-team-standing survival sabotage.
  * 🎃 **PumpongUs:** A social deduction twist featuring pumpkin heads, sabotages, goo tentacles, and emergency meetings!

## ⚡ Game Features & Systems

### 🔫 Arsenal & Weapon Systems
* **Diverse Armory:** Dozens of authentic firearms ranging from starter pistols, SMGs, and assault rifles to shotguns, heavy machine guns, and sniper rifles.
* **Wall Buys:** Purchase staple weapons directly off chalk outlines and restock ammunition between rounds.
* **The Mystery Box:** Roll the box for a chance at high-tier firepower or coveted **Wonder Weapons** (such as the *Raygun* and *Raygun Mk II*).
* **Pack-a-Punch Machine:**
  * Major weapon damage boost and expanded magazine capacity.
  * Distinct custom Pack-a-Punch weapon skin and firing sounds.
  * **Alternate Ammo Types (Repack):** Re-pack weapons to trigger elemental procs like **Blast Furnace** (firestorm AOE) or **Electric** (chain lightning).

---

### 🥤 Perk-a-Cola Vending Machines
* **Juggernog:** Boosts maximum survivor health from 100 to 250 HP.
* **Quick Revive:** Dramatically halves ally revive times; grants self-revive in solo play.
* **Speed Cola:** Significantly increases weapon reload and cycling speed.
* **Double Tap Root Beer:** Increases bullet damage and increases fire rate.
* **Stamin-Up:** Enhances sprint duration and movement speed to outrun fast sprinters.
* **Mule Kick / Three Gun:** Grants the ability to carry a third primary weapon.
* **Der Wunderfizz:** Dynamic orb machine dispensing random perks across the map.
* **Secret Prone Easter Egg:** Prone in front of perk machines for 100 bonus starting points!

---

### 💥 Tactical Powerup Drops
* 💀 **Insta-Kill:** All zombie damage becomes instantly lethal for 30 seconds.
* 📦 **Max Ammo:** Instantly refills all ammo pools for all players.
* 💣 **Nuke:** Obliterates all active zombies on the map and awards bonus cash.
* 🔨 **Carpenter:** Instantly repairs all window barricades across the entire facility.
* 💰 **Double Points (x2):** Multiplies all point gains for a limited duration.
* 🚀 **Death Machine:** Equips a heavy devastating minigun with endless firepower.

---

### 🧠 Smart AI Companions & Solo Support
* **Full Autonomous Bot Support:** Play solo or fill empty lobby slots with intelligent AI teammates.
* **Goal-Oriented Behavior:** Bots navigate complex maps, buy weapons, repair boards, purchase perks, use Pack-a-Punch, collect powerups, and actively prioritize reviving downed allies.

---

### 🚗 Interactive Map Utilities & World Mechanics
* **Electric Generators & Teleporters:** Route power through the map and teleport to Pack A Punch using the teleporter pad (*Kino Der Toten*).
* **Acoustic Noise System:** Comprehensive natural and exponential sound falloff calculation—stealth and trigger discipline matter.
* **Downed & Revive Mechanic:** 3D revive rings and bleedout indicators so no teammate is left behind.
